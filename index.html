The Gym

Nearly 40 Years On Commercial Street

Since 1983, Lewis Boxing Gym has stood at 434 W Commercial St in Springfield, Missouri — a true old school boxing gym with all the basics you need to actually learn the sport. No frills, no fads. Just heavy bags, ring time, and real coaching.

Over the years the gym has produced amateur and professional fighters alike, with ten pro boxers having come up through its doors. But most who walk in aren't chasing a title — they're here to get in shape, learn to fight, and become part of a gym that welcomes everyone.

The door is open to all ages, from 9 to 75, and every demographic in Springfield. If you've driven past on Commercial Street and wondered what's inside, the answer is simple: a real boxing gym.

Howell Lewis has run Lewis Boxing Gym for nearly forty years, training everyone from first-timers to professional fighters. His approach is simple: old school fundamentals, real work, and a gym door that's open to everyone who walks through it.
